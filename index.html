The Counter

A family-owned counter, not a chain.

Dugagjini Meats has held its spot on Riopelle Street since 1948. The premium quality cuts are served at prices maintaining consistency and fairness throughout all of Michigan.

The case goes beyond the usual fresh cuts: seafood (jumbo shrimp, king crab legs, snow crab legs, ocean perch, sardines), poultry, dairy, and seasoning sit alongside cured specialties like prosciutto, salami, and ham. Halal meats are available year-round, with the full range especially highlighted around Ramadan. Everything is sold retail or wholesale, with delivery to restaurants and other businesses.

Good to Know

Common questions, answered plainly.

Is the meat halal? Halal meats are available at the counter, with the full range especially highlighted during Ramadan
Do you sell wholesale? Yes, retail and wholesale alike, with delivery available to restaurants and other businesses.
Can I get a custom cut? Yes you can! Staff at the counter will tend to your very needs.
Is it just meat? We also carry seafood (shrimp, crab legs, ocean perch, sardines), dairy, and seasonings, alongside cured deli items like prosciutto and salami.
